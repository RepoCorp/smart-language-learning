{source_text}

{word_type}

{notes}

1. Up to 5 words from the same word family.
2. Up to 5 related, contrasting, or commonly confused words.

Requirements:

- Every generated word must be appropriate for the student's level unless {notes} specifies otherwise.

- Keep the sentences short and use common everyday vocabulary.
- Do not repeat nearly identical sentences.
- Do not include grammar explanations.
