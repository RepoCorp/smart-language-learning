The target noun must be in the accusative case.

Prefer a short, natural sentence in which the target noun is the direct object of the verb.

Choose a verb whose action is strongly and naturally associated with the target noun.

The German question should naturally identify the target noun using **Wen?** for a person or **Was?** for a thing.

Examples:

* Ich sehe den Hund. → Wen sehe ich?
* Ich öffne die Tür. → Was öffne ich?
* Ich höre die Musik. → Was höre ich?

If using the target noun as a direct object would produce an unnatural or contrived sentence, use another common and natural construction that requires the accusative case. Never force the direct-object pattern at the expense of natural German.

Do not apply the requested determiner to the subject or to any other noun in the sentence.
