The target noun must be in the nominative case and be the grammatical subject of the sentence.

Generate the shortest natural sentence in which the target noun performs a characteristic action or is in a characteristic state.

Prefer an action or state that strongly evokes the meaning of the noun.

The German question must naturally identify the subject using **Wer?** for a person or **Was?** for a thing.

Examples:

* Der Hund bellt. → Wer bellt?
* Die Erfahrung zählt. → Was zählt?
* Das Feuer brennt. → Was brennt?
