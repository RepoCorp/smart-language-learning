# English word entries

When English is the target language, standalone noun entries use the singular
noun with the definite article (the dog). Plural forms also include the article
(the dogs). Verb entries use to followed by the infinitive (to work, to be,
to look after).

These conventions apply to word metadata generation and keyword extraction,
including newly added and regenerated words. They do not rewrite existing saved
items, expressions, source-language conventions, or natural example sentences.

Language-specific prompts live under word_metadata/english and items/english.
